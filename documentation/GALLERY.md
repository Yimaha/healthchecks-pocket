# Gallery

Healthchecks Pocket on Android, with the built-in demo project (paste `demo` as the API key to try it yourself).

## In use

From the check list to a check, then a failed run's ping log, and back.

<img src="../docs/media/walkthrough.gif" alt="The app in use: the check list, then a check, then a failed run's ping log, and back" width="320">

## Check list

Every check with its icon and status, down and late first, with a summary of how many are down, late and up, and
tags to filter by.

<img src="../docs/media/check-list.png" alt="The check list: each check with its icon and status, with a summary of how many are down, late and up" width="320">

## A check

Its status, schedule, uptime and tags, the times it went down and up, and its latest pings.

<img src="../docs/media/check-detail.png" alt="A check: its status, schedule, uptime, status changes and ping log" width="320">

## Ping log

What a run sent, with errors in red and debug lines dimmed. Copy or share it, change the text size, or turn off
wrapping.

<img src="../docs/media/ping-body.png" alt="A failed run's ping log, with the errors in red" width="320">

## Settings

Projects, appearance, where API keys are kept, and update checks.

<img src="../docs/media/settings.png" alt="Settings: projects, appearance, API key protection and update checks" width="320">

## Heartbeat monitor background

One of the backgrounds to pick in Settings: a heartbeat trace, drawn the way a bedside monitor draws it, beating at
a resting heart rate. Shown in the dark theme, then the light one.

<img src="../docs/media/heartbeat.gif" alt="The heartbeat monitor background behind the check list, in the dark theme and then the light one" width="320">
